ABOUT

I am a PhD student in the School of Educational Technology at Beijing Normal University [Our Lab]. My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIEd) and Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT). Guided by the belief that "every child born has innate goodness", no matter how technology evolves, I believe technology will always be beneficial to education when designed with care.
