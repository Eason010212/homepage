About me

I am a PhD student in the School of Educational Technology at Beijing Normal University (Our Lab). My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIEd) and Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT). Guided by the belief that “every child born has innate goodness”, I focus on designing human-centric AI-assisted assessment systems that empower learners and teachers, rather than replacing them.

Research interests

  • Automatic Item Generation
  • Educational Data Mining
  • LLM Benchmarking
  • Computerized Adaptive Testing

A textbook series serving 1.2 million students in primary, junior high, and senior high schools across Heilongjiang Province, China.
